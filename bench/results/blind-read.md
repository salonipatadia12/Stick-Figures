## Why the sky is blue

6. Sunlight hits these tiny molecules and gets tossed around.
7. So blue light jumps everywhere while red passes through.
8. This special bouncing by the gas makes clear days look like a blue dome above us.
9. But the sun's blues scatter sideways for us, leaving red ahead.
10. Rayleigh scattering makes shorter ones bounce harder than longer wavelengths make it through our atmosphere.
11. Look again at that blue sky.
