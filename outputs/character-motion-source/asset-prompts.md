# Asset generation prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. Source: user-approved
`outputs/character-focus-reference.png` (941×1672).

## Café plate

Edit the exact image into a clean background plate for local 2D animation. Remove
only the entire white/navy stick character, including head, torso, arms, fingers and
held pen. Reconstruct the occluded café/window/chair/table naturally. Keep the open
notebook, mug, table, chair, window, pendant lights, foliage, framing and warm light.
Notebook remains blank. Preserve portrait aspect and perspective; no zoom, crop,
recentring, added objects, people, pen or text.

## Foreground cutout

Extract the exact character as an RGBA layer on genuine transparency. Keep the
complete head and unchanged face, white shaded torso, both dark arms, both hands,
fingers and the single held teal pen. Preserve warm edge lighting and design detail.
Remove every background element, mug, notebook, chair, table and cast shadow. Keep
full portrait canvas, scale, pose and positions; no tight crop or recentering. Gaps
between limbs and torso must be transparent. No replacement limbs or redesign.

## Body base

Edit the transparent cutout. Remove only the writing arm on the left side of the
image: shoulder-to-elbow arm stroke, forearm across the torso, gripping hand and pen.
Reconstruct the occluded white torso, shaded edge and continuous navy outline. Keep
head/expression and the other arm/resting hand unchanged. Preserve the 941×1672
canvas, exact remaining positions and true transparency. No replacement writing arm.
