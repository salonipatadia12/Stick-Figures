The video is drawn as minimal black-ink line art on near-white paper. The look is adapted from style B1 of kaomei's Stickman Video Director (MIT).

**Characters (cast ids).**
- `lead`: the host. A slender stick figure drawn in fine, slightly irregular black pen: a modest round head, a long single-line torso and limbs, small oval hands and feet, about four heads tall. Black oval eyes, strong brows and a mouth that changes shape with every feeling.
- `friend`: the same slender figure with a short marker-red scarf. Use the friend only when a second person helps the idea, such as a contrast, a question or a reaction.

The characters act with the whole body. Gaze, head tilt, lean, arm reach and stance change together. Push expressions further than feels natural: wide surprise, open-mouthed frustration, squinting strain, a broad grin, a deflated frown. Do not reuse one face across the video. Hands really touch what they hold, and feet are planted.

**World.** Near-white warm paper with an extremely faint square grid, never parchment and never heavy texture. Every place is a simple ink drawing: a few lines for a room, a street or a lab, and one recognizable object drawn in enough detail to explain the idea. Leave plenty of empty paper.

**Colour.** Black ink, plus at most three marker accents across the whole video, each with one meaning:
- warm yellow: discovery, the idea landing
- marker red: a problem, a warning, an idea ruled out
- leaf green: the answer, things going right

**How to show abstract ideas here.** Diagrams are hand-drawn and part of the page: curved arrows, a sketched flow of three boxes, a circled detail, a crossed-out belief, a little drawn meter or clock. A key object can carry an idea from one beat to the next.

**Text.** Large, short hand-lettered words are the focal point of many frames. The compositor adds them from on_screen_text, so never ask for words inside the drawing. Keep them to 1-4 words. Captions sit quietly below in a plain paper band.

**Shots.** Mostly the host acting beside one drawn object, plus character-free close-ups of the object and short reading holds. Use match cuts on shapes, and carry a prop between ideas. Let a move settle before the next one. Do not cut faster just to look busy.

**Sound.** No music. A few restrained action sounds (a pen scratch, a pop, a thud), always below the voice.

**Tone.** Clear, curious and a little playful.
