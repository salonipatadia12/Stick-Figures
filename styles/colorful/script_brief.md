The video is hand-drawn in full colour on warm cream paper, and every idea gets its own place. The look is adapted from style B2 of kaomei's Stickman Video Director (MIT).

**Characters (cast ids).**
- `lead`: the host. An ordinary round-headed stick figure with sparse dot eyes and a short line mouth. Its body is filled and wears simple, colourful clothes that suit the story: a raincoat in the rain, an apron in a kitchen.
- `friend`: a second round-headed figure whose clothes are a different colour. Use the friend only when a second person helps the idea.

Pose, gaze, a small expression and the story context give the characters their identity. Poses are stable and readable. Characters do not need to walk or wave all the time, and constant limb movement is not polish. A held phone or key is really touched, and an object never jumps between hands or passes through a table.

**World.** Full-colour, muted but varied hand-drawn places with fine, slightly irregular ink lines and simple graphic depth. **Every idea gets its own place**: a shop counter, a bus, a kitchen, a park, a workplace, a close-up of the object itself. Never use the same room with swapped props, and never default to a standing presenter in a plain room. Reusing a place is fine when the story returns to it on purpose; keep its layout and props the same when it does.

**How to show abstract ideas here.** Specific everyday actions, an amusing visual contradiction, a diagram drawn as an object (a labelled jar, a calendar, a receipt), and a concrete payoff. Person-free close-ups of an object are welcome when a body adds nothing.

**Text.** Handwritten scene headings, added by the compositor from on_screen_text and never inside the picture. Captions are readable sans-serif in dark ink on a small ivory paper label at the bottom.

**Sound.** Restrained action sounds below the narration. An optional light acoustic bed, off by default.

**Tone.** Warm, relatable, gently funny. Never preachy. No psychology labels or medical claims the facts do not support.
