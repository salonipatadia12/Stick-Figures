The video looks like an old television broadcast: white and gray figures glowing on a dark field. The look is adapted from style B3 of kaomei's Stickman Video Director (MIT).

**Characters (cast ids).**
- `lead`: the host. A refined white figure with a blank circular head, rounded joints and clear gaps between arms and body. It has no eyes, nose or mouth drawn in, so the body tells the feeling: a slumped back, a hand raised, a lean toward something.
- `friend`: the same build in light gray, so the two read apart. Use the friend only when a second person helps the idea.

Poses are stable and readable, with small, purposeful reactions. If the narration names a behaviour (steps back, freezes, looks up), the picture shows it happening. Constant fidgeting does not count as motion.

**World.** A deep dark field with lots of empty space. Places and objects are drawn simply in white and soft gray: a doorway, a window, a desk, a sky drawn as a few lines. Each beat has one dominant subject. Vary the place and the viewpoint from idea to idea, not the same room with new props.

**Colour.** White and gray, plus at most one signal-red accent for a warning, a strike-through or the moment something goes wrong. A gentle explainer may not need the red at all.

**How to show abstract ideas here.** Use the tools this look is good at:
- progressive line diagrams that draw themselves
- a framed screen-in-screen insert (a monitor, a feed) whose picture changes
- an object or screen that changes state
- a connector line that links two things
- a brief signal interruption on a story turn

Do not force a red warning or a glitch into a calm topic.

**Text.** Sparse, compact uppercase headings for the key word, added by the compositor from on_screen_text (never inside the picture). Captions are white uppercase at the bottom.

**Finish.** A restrained old-TV pass over the finished frame: soft bloom, faint scanlines and a gentle vignette. It is applied once, after the animation already reads cleanly. It is never a substitute for action.

**Sound.** A quiet ambient bed, ducked under the voice. A few restrained action sounds timed to real events. Signal noise is an occasional accent, never the soundtrack.

**Tone.** Calm, slightly mysterious, observational.
