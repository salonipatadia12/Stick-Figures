Sam hadn't seen his friend in months.

He typed, ‘Want to grab a coffee?’ Then deleted it. They were probably busy.

A week later, his friend messaged. ‘It's been a rough week.’

This time, Sam didn't overthink it. ‘Coffee tomorrow? My treat.’

The reply came straight back. ‘I'd really like that.’

Two coffees. One long conversation.

Turns out, his friend had been waiting to reach out, too.
