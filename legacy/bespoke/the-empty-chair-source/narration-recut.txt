Every morning, she drew at the same café. And every morning, her bag took the other chair.

Until someone asked, “Mind if I sit?”

She hesitated... then moved it.

He noticed her sketchbook. “Wait... you drew this?”

They talked until their coffee went cold.

The next morning, she saved his seat.

Then the next.

By the fourth morning, the bag was back.

That's when she heard, “I tried drawing our table.”

He held up a sketchbook. Crooked legs. Cups like potatoes.

She laughed, moved her bag... and pulled out his chair.
