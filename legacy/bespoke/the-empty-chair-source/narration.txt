Every morning, she sat at the same café table. Her sketchbook on the table. Her bag on the other chair.

One day, a man asked, “Is this seat taken?”

She hesitated... then moved her bag.

They sat quietly, until he noticed her drawing.

“You drew this place?”

She nodded. And they started talking.

The next morning, she left his chair empty.

But he didn't come.

For three days, she watched the door.

On the fourth, she put her bag back.

Then she heard, “I tried drawing our table.”

He was holding a sketchbook. The table was crooked. The cups looked like potatoes.

She laughed, moved her bag... and pulled out his chair.
