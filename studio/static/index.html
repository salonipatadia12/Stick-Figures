Your studio setup

For Claude story drafting, add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and STORY_PROVIDER=anthropic to your server’s .env, then restart using --env-file .env and refresh this page. OpenAI story drafting remains available with OPENAI_API_KEY and STORY_PROVIDER=openai. Your key stays on the server. AI requests may incur provider charges. Animated characters and preset backgrounds need no image API. New AI illustrations require an OpenAI key, or you can upload your own artwork.

You can also write a story yourself and upload illustrations and voice recordings without an AI key. The finished example uses existing files and makes no generation requests.

See studio/README.md for the local setup and Docker server instructions. Reusable animation includes twelve actions, six settings, independent background uploads and audio-responsive mouths. It uses a fixed action library, not arbitrary choreography or phoneme-level lip sync. Story JSON exports the script and directions; keep uploaded images and recordings separately.
